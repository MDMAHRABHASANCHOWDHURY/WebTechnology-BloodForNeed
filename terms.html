Terms & Conditions

By Using The Blood For Need Platform, You Agree To The Following Terms And Conditions. These Rules Are Created To Ensure Ethical, Safe, And Transparent Blood Donation Practices Across Bangladesh.

1. Voluntary Participation

Blood Donation Through This Platform Is Completely Voluntary. No User Is Forced Or Obligated To Donate Blood.

2. Eligibility Of Donors

Donors Must Meet Standard Medical Eligibility Requirements, Including Age, Weight, And Health Condition, Before Donating Blood.

3. Accuracy Of Information

Users Must Provide Correct And Truthful Personal And Medical Information. Providing False Information May Lead To Account Suspension.

4. Use Of Personal Data

Approved Medical Staff Can View Donor Contact Information For Donation Coordination. Public Hospital Cards Hide Patient Names And Donor Contacts. Your Profile Photograph May Be Viewed By Signed-in Medical Staff And Administrators.

5. No Financial Transactions

Blood For Need Does Not Support Or Allow Buying Or Selling Of Blood. Any Form Of Financial Demand Related To Blood Donation Is Strictly Prohibited.

6. Medical Responsibility

Blood For Need Acts Only As A Connecting Platform. It Is Not Responsible For Any Medical Complications, Transfusion Outcomes, Or Health-related Issues.

7. Respectful Conduct

All Users Must Communicate Respectfully. Harassment, Abuse, Or Inappropriate Behavior Will Not Be Tolerated.

8. Emergency Situations

While The Platform Helps Connect Donors And Recipients, It Does Not Guarantee Immediate Donor Availability In Emergency Cases.

9. Account Suspension

Blood For Need Reserves The Right To Suspend Or Permanently Remove Any Account That Violates These Terms And Conditions.

10. Changes To Terms

These Terms And Conditions May Be Updated At Any Time. Continued Use Of The Platform Means Acceptance Of The Updated Terms.

11. Acceptance Of Terms

By Accessing Or Using This Platform, You Confirm That You Have Read, Understood, And Agreed To All The Above Terms And Conditions.
